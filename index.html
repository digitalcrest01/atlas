What's inside

Explore by ear and by eye

Free

A spoken tour of every country

Two or three minutes that feel like a local friend showing you around: everyday life, food, celebrations and landscapes.

It is told in the country's own language by a natural, human-sounding voice, with a small English translation under every line.

  • Photos of the places being described change as the story goes on
  • The country's flag rises from the globe while the rest of the world fades to grey
  • Replay, pause and pick up where you left off
Free

A living 3D globe

A photorealistic Earth that keeps turning, with every country's flag on it. Drag to spin it, tap a country to fly there, or switch the flags off for a clear view.

  • Capital, currency, languages, local time, culture, history and food for all 195 countries
  • A soft background sound while you explore, which falls quiet whenever something is spoken
Free

A phrasebook you can hear

Pick a phrase, such as hello, thank you or how much?, and hear it said by a local voice, with the English meaning and a simple guide to saying it yourself.

  • More than 60 languages, written in their own scripts
  • Filipino (Tagalog), Swahili, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese and many more
Free

Passports of the world

Pick any country to see its real passport cover. Every visit earns an immigration-style entry stamp, and the more you explore, the fuller your passport gets.

  • Official covers from each country's passport, where a free image exists
  • Stamps in different inks and shapes, dated by visit

  • Globe Flag Hunt and adjustable difficulty

Made for curious kids

A bright, playful Kids mode, reading levels for ages 3–5, 6–8 and 9–12, and a dyslexia-friendly reading option. Grown-ups love it too.
